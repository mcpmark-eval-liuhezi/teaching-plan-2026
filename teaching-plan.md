# Teaching Plan — First Draft (Semester 2026)

> Status: **Draft v0.1** — prepared as a follow-up to the Academic Committee Meeting.
> This outline is a starting point for discussion. All topics and weights are open for revision.

---

## 1. Course Goals

By the end of the semester, students should be able to:

1. **Understand core concepts** — explain the foundational theory covered across the weekly modules.
2. **Apply methods in practice** — carry the course techniques through to hands-on exercises and a project.
3. **Analyse and evaluate** — read, critique, and compare approaches using appropriate criteria and evidence.
4. **Communicate results** — present technical work clearly in both written reports and short presentations.
5. **Work collaboratively** — contribute to team deliverables and give/receive structured peer feedback.

## 2. Weekly Topics (indicative)

| Week | Topic | Focus |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Course overview & expectations | Logistics, assessment rubric, team formation |
| 2 | Foundations I | Core concepts and terminology |
| 3 | Foundations II | Key techniques and worked examples |
| 4 | Methods & tools | Tooling walkthrough, lab session |
| 5 | Case study | Guided analysis of a real example |
| 6 | Assignment 1 workshop | In-class support for first assessment |
| 7 | Mid-term review | Consolidation, Q&A, feedback on progress |
| 8 | Advanced topic I | Extension material, recent developments |
| 9 | Advanced topic II | Comparative approaches and trade-offs |
| 10 | Project proposal | Teams present project scope and plan |
| 11 | Project clinic | Troubleshooting, data/method issues |
| 12 | Student presentations I | Team presentations and peer review |
| 13 | Student presentations II | Team presentations and peer review |
| 14 | Review & exam preparation | Summary, exam format, revision guidance |

## 3. Assessment

| Component | Weight | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Assignment 1 (individual) | 15% | Weeks 2–6 material, due end of Week 6 |
| Assignment 2 (individual) | 20% | Advanced topics, due end of Week 10 |
| Team project & presentation | 30% | Proposal (Week 10), final presentation (Weeks 12–13) |
| Final exam | 35% | End-of-semester, closed-book |

*Grading also reflects participation in peer-review sessions (bonus, up to 5%, at instructor discretion).*
